Situation

The problem

Sellers struggled, and so did the people supporting them

Sellers struggled

The third-party flow was called confusing and not intuitive. Some sellers had to force themselves through it.

Business Developers overloaded

Hours spent answering repetitive questions and troubleshooting instead of growing the marketplace.

Revenue left on the table

Average time for completing onboarding was one month.

1 mo

average time to complete onboarding

70

new sellers: the 2024 KPI, put at risk because sellers could not start selling promptly

Task

My role

Two parts, one onboarding flow

Part A: Commercial onboarding

Go through the existing process, design steps. Ran continuous user tests with sellers and worked with Business Developers to find and fix pain points.

Part B: Technical onboarding

Led end-to-end design from scratch with a fellow designer: test analysis, UX solutions, dev-ready handoff.

Tools: Figma, Miro, Excel, Jira, Confluence
